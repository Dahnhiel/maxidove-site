FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our federal contracting services.

How quickly can you prepare a federal proposal? +

Turnaround time depends on the size and complexity of the RFP. Most proposals can be completed within 7–14 business days. We also accommodate tight deadlines when needed.

Do you guarantee we will win the contract? +

No reputable firm can guarantee a win, as awards are decided by the government agency. However, our criteria-focused approach has helped clients achieve an average win rate of 76%.

What sectors do you work with? +

We support companies across IT & Technology, Construction, Engineering, Healthcare, Logistics, Professional Services, Facilities Management, and many other sectors.

How does your pricing work? +

We offer fixed-fee pricing for individual bids and monthly retainer options for ongoing support. Pricing is based on the scope of work. The initial consultation is always free.

Can you help companies with no prior federal experience? +

Yes. We specialize in helping first-time federal contractors understand the process, build strong capability statements, and submit competitive bids.
