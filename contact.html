FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our tender and government contract consultancy.

How quickly can you turn around a bid? +

Turnaround depends on the complexity and length of the tender. Most ITT responses can be drafted within 5–10 working days. For urgent submissions we can accommodate faster timelines — get in touch to discuss your deadline.

Do you guarantee a win? +

No ethical consultancy can guarantee a contract award — public procurement decisions are made by the contracting authority. However, our structured, criteria-led approach significantly improves your chances. Our current client win rate stands at 78%.

What sectors do you specialise in? +

We work across a wide range of sectors including construction, IT & technology, healthcare and social care, facilities management, professional services, training, and consultancy — to name a few.

How does pricing work? +

We offer both fixed-fee project pricing and monthly retainer packages. Fees depend on the scope and complexity of support required. Contact us for a tailored quote — the initial consultation is always free.

Can you help if we've never bid before? +

Absolutely. We work with businesses at all stages — from first-time bidders looking to understand the process, to established companies wanting to increase their win rate on a specific framework or portal.
